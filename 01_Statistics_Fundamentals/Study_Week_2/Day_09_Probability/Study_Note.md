# 📘 Day 9 Study Note — Probability

## 🎯 Objective
Calculate simple and conditional probabilities.

---

### 🧠 Key Concepts

## Probability
Probability measures the likelihood of an event occurring.

Formula:
P(A) = Favorable Outcomes / Total Outcomes

---

### Conditional Probability

Conditional probability measures the likelihood of an event occurring given another event has already occurred.

Formula:
P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)

---

## 💻 Excel Logic

Simple Probability:
=COUNTIF(range,criteria)/total

Conditional Probability:
=COUNTIFS(range1,criteria1,range2,criteria2)/COUNTIF(range,criteria)

---

## 📌 Important Notes

✅ Probability values range from 0 to 1.

✅ Conditional probability depends on an existing condition.

✅ Probability helps quantify uncertainty.
