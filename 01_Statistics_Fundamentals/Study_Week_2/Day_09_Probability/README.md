# 🎲 Day 9 — Probability & Conditional Probability

## 📘 Objective
Understand how probability quantifies likelihood and supports analytical decision-making.

---

## 📚 Concepts Covered
- Simple Probability
- Conditional Probability
- Probability Formula
- Event-Based Reasoning

---

## 🛠 Analysis Performed
- Calculated probability of high-priority support tickets
- Calculated probability of tickets arriving via email
- Computed conditional probability of high-priority tickets given email channel

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- High-priority tickets represented a minority of all support tickets.
- Email accounted for a substantial share of support requests.
- Email tickets had a higher probability of being high-priority relative to the general ticket pool.

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaway
Conditional probability enables more targeted analysis by evaluating events within specific subsets of data.
