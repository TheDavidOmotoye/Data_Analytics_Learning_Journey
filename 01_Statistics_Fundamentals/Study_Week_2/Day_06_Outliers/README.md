# 🚨 Day 6 — Outlier Detection

## 📘 Objective
Learn how to identify and interpret unusual observations within a dataset using the IQR method.

---

## 📚 Concepts Covered
- Outliers
- Interquartile Range (IQR)
- Lower and Upper Bounds
- Box Plot Interpretation

---

## 🛠 Analysis Performed
- Calculated Q1, Q3, and IQR for transaction dataset
- Determined lower and upper outlier bounds
- Flagged extreme values outside accepted range
- Interpreted business significance of anomalies

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Multiple transaction outliers were detected using the IQR method.
- Outliers may represent fraud, data entry issues, exceptional transactions, or business anomalies.
- Outliers should be investigated rather than removed automatically.

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaway
Outliers are not inherently bad data; they often signal important exceptions that require business investigation.
