## 📖 Statistical Concepts Applied

## 📌 Central Tendency

### Mean
Mean = Σx / n

Excel:
=AVERAGE(range)

### Median
=MEDIAN(range)

### Mode
=MODE.SNGL(range)

---

## 📌 Variability Analysis

### Range
Range = Maximum − Minimum

### Standard Deviation
s = √[Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1)]

Excel:
=MAX(range)-MIN(range)

=STDEV.S(range)

---

## 📌 Outlier Detection

### IQR Formula
IQR = Q3 − Q1

### Lower Bound
Q1 − 1.5(IQR)

### Upper Bound
Q3 + 1.5(IQR)

Used to identify unusually high or low observations.

---

## 📌 Correlation Analysis

### Correlation Formula
r = Σ[(x − x̄)(y − ȳ)] / √[Σ(x − x̄)² Σ(y − ȳ)²]

Excel:
=CORREL(x_range,y_range)

## 📊 Key Analysis Performed

## 1️⃣ Salary Analysis
- Calculated average salary
- Evaluated salary spread
- Identified salary distribution pattern

### Key Finding
Salary distribution showed positive skewness due to higher earners.

---

## 2️⃣ Performance Analysis

Compared:
- training hours,
- absenteeism,
- and project completion

against performance scores.

### Key Findings

| Variables | Correlation |
|---|---|
| Training vs Performance | 0.87 |
| Absent Days vs Performance | -0.80 |
| Projects vs Performance | 0.33 |

Interpretation:
- More training improved performance.
- Higher absenteeism reduced performance.
- Projects completed had moderate impact.

---

## 3️⃣ Outlier Detection

Detected unusually high and low salary observations using the IQR method.

Purpose:
- identify anomalies,
- investigate inconsistencies,
- improve data reliability.
