## Key Insights
* Training hours showed a strong positive relationship with performance score (r ≈ 0.87), suggesting training contributes significantly to performance.
* Tenure had only a weak-to-moderate relationship with salary, implying salary progression is not purely tenure-based.
* Absent days had negligible correlation with performance, indicating attendance alone was not a strong predictor of output.
* Salary distribution contained notable high-end outliers, suggesting a small number of highly compensated employees.
* Departmental analysis showed IT had the highest average salary, while Sales achieved the highest performance score.
* Combined statistical analysis revealed workforce performance is influenced more by capability development than by tenure or attendance alone.
