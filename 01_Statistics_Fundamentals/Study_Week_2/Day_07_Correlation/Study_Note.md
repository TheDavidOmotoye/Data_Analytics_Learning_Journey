# 📘 Day 7 Study Note — Correlation

## 🎯 Objective
Measure relationship strength between two variables.

---

## 🧠 Key Concepts

## Correlation
Correlation measures how strongly two variables move together.

---

## 📌 Correlation Coefficient (r)

Range of correlation values:

| Correlation Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| +1 | Perfect Positive Relationship |
| 0 | No Relationship |
| -1 | Perfect Negative Relationship |

---

## 📌 Correlation Formula

r = Σ[(x − x̄)(y − ȳ)] / √[Σ(x − x̄)² Σ(y − ȳ)²]

---

## 💻 Excel Formula

=CORREL(x_range,y_range)

---

## 📈 Dataset Result

Correlation coefficient:
r = 0.996

Interpretation:
- Extremely strong positive relationship.

As advertising spend increases, leads generated also increase significantly.

---

## 📊 Scatter Plot Observation

The scatter plot showed:
- Strong upward trend
- Linear relationship
- Minimal dispersion

---

## 📌 Important Notes

✅ Correlation measures association, not causation.

✅ Strong correlations may help forecasting and prediction.

✅ Scatter plots help visualize relationships.

---

## 🔍 Key Insight

Correlation helps businesses:
- Predict trends
- Optimize marketing spend
- Understand business drivers
- Support strategic decisions

---

Correlation transforms relationships into measurable statistics, making trend analysis clearer and more reliable.
