# 🔗 Day 7 — Correlation Analysis

## 📘 Objective
Understand how to measure and interpret relationships between two quantitative variables.

---

## 📚 Concepts Covered
- Correlation Coefficient (r)
- Positive vs Negative Correlation
- Strength of Correlation
- Correlation vs Causation

---

## 🛠 Analysis Performed
- Calculated correlation manually and using Excel CORREL()
- Created scatter plot for ad spend vs leads generated
- Validated relationship visually and statistically
- Checked dataset for outliers prior to correlation analysis

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Advertising spend and leads generated showed a very strong positive correlation (**r = 0.996**).
- Increased advertising spend was strongly associated with increased lead generation.
- The relationship appeared linear and highly consistent.

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaway
Correlation quantifies relationships between variables but should never be mistaken for proof of causation.
