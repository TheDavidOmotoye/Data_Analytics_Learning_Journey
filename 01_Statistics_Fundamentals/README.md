## 📖 Statistical Concepts Learned

## 📌 Measures of Central Tendency

### Mean
Mean = Σx / n

Excel Formula:
=AVERAGE(range)

---

### Median

Excel Formula:
=MEDIAN(range)

---

### Mode

Excel Formula:
=MODE.SNGL(range)

---

## 📌 Measures of Variability

### Range
Range = Maximum − Minimum

Excel Formula:
=MAX(range)-MIN(range)

---

### Variance
s² = Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1)

Excel Formula:
=VAR.S(range)

---

### Standard Deviation
s = √[Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1)]

Excel Formula:
=STDEV.S(range)

---

## 📌 Quartiles & Percentiles

### Interquartile Range (IQR)
IQR = Q3 − Q1

Excel Formulas:
=QUARTILE.INC(range,1)

=QUARTILE.INC(range,3)

=PERCENTILE.INC(range,k)

---

## 📌 Outlier Detection

### Lower Bound
Q1 − 1.5(IQR)

### Upper Bound
Q3 + 1.5(IQR)

Outliers are values:
- below the lower bound,
- or above the upper bound.

---

## 📌 Correlation

### Correlation Formula
r = Σ[(x − x̄)(y − ȳ)] / √[Σ(x − x̄)² Σ(y − ȳ)²]

Excel Formula:
=CORREL(x_range,y_range)

---

### Correlation Interpretation

| Correlation Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| +1 | Perfect Positive Relationship |
| 0 | No Relationship |
| -1 | Perfect Negative Relationship |

---

## 📌 Probability

### Simple Probability
P(A) = Favorable Outcomes / Total Outcomes

### Conditional Probability
P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)

Excel Formulas:
=COUNTIF(range,criteria)/total

=COUNTIFS(range1,criteria1,range2,criteria2)/COUNTIF(range,criteria)
