# 📘 Day 4 Study Note — Quartiles & Percentiles

## 🎯 Objective
Understand how data is distributed and positioned.

---

## 🧠 Key Concepts

## Quartiles

Quartiles divide data into four equal parts.

| Quartile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Q1 | 25% of data below |
| Q2 | Median (50%) |
| Q3 | 75% of data below |

---

## Interquartile Range (IQR)

Formula:
IQR = Q3 − Q1

---

## Percentiles

Percentiles divide data into 100 equal parts.

Example:
- 90th percentile means 90% of values fall below that point.

---

## 💻 Excel Functions

=QUARTILE.INC(range,1)
=MEDIAN(range)
=QUARTILE.INC(range,3)
=PERCENTILE.INC(range,0.9)

---

## 📊 Results

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Q1 | 72.75 |
| Median | 81.5 |
| Q3 | 89.25 |
| IQR | 16.5 |
| 90th Percentile | 95.1 |

---

## 📌 Important Insight

Quartiles help identify:
- Spread
- Position
- Outliers
- Data concentration

---

## 📝 Reflection

Quartiles simplify large datasets by showing how values are distributed across sections of the data.
