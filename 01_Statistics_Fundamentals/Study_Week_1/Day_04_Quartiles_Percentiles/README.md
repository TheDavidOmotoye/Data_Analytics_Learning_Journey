# 📊 Day 4 — Quartiles & Percentiles

## 📘 Objective
Learn how to divide data into ranked segments for positional analysis.

---

## 📚 Concepts Covered
- Quartiles
- Percentiles
- Interquartile Range (IQR)
- Positional Interpretation

---

## 🛠 Analysis Performed
- Calculated Q1, Median, and Q3
- Computed selected percentiles
- Interpreted percentile rankings in practical scenarios

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Quartiles divide datasets into four equal ranked portions.
- Percentiles show the relative standing of a value within a distribution.
- IQR measures the spread of the middle 50% of observations.

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaway
Quartiles and percentiles provide context beyond averages by revealing where values sit within the broader distribution.
