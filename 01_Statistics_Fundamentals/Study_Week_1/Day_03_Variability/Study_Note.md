# 📘 Day 3 Study Note — Range, Variance & Standard Deviation

## 🎯 Objective
Measure data variability and determine consistency.

---

## 🧠 Key Concepts

## 1️⃣ Range
Difference between maximum and minimum values.

Formula:
Range = Maximum - Minimum

Excel Formula:
=MAX(range)-MIN(range)

---

## 2️⃣ Variance
Measures how far values spread from the mean.

Formula:
s² = Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1)

Excel Formula:
=VAR.S(range)

---

## 3️⃣ Standard Deviation
Square root of variance.

Formula:
s = √[Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1)]

Excel Formula:
=STDEV.S(range)

---

## 📊 Interpretation

| Store | Std. Dev. | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Store A | Lowest | Most consistent |
| Store B | Moderate | Moderate variation |
| Store C | Highest | Least consistent |

---

## 📌 Important Notes

✅ Smaller standard deviation means more consistency.

✅ Higher variance means greater spread in data.

✅ Standard deviation is easier to interpret because it uses original units.

---

## 🔍 Key Insight

Variability measures help businesses understand:
- Risk
- Stability
- Consistency
- Predictability

---

## 📝 Reflection

Not all datasets with similar averages behave similarly. Variability reveals the hidden spread within the data.
