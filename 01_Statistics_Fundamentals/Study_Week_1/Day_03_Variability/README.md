# 📈 Day 3 — Measures of Variability

## 📘 Objective
Understand how spread and dispersion are measured within a dataset.

---

## 📚 Concepts Covered
- Range
- Variance
- Standard Deviation
- Population vs Sample Metrics

---

## 🛠 Analysis Performed
- Calculated range, variance, and standard deviation for practice datasets
- Compared data consistency across datasets
- Interpreted variability in business contexts

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Variability reveals how dispersed observations are around the mean.
- Higher standard deviation indicates greater inconsistency.
- Standard deviation is more interpretable than variance due to unit consistency.

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaway
Understanding spread is essential because identical averages can mask dramatically different data distributions.
