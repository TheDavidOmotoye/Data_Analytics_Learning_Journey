# 📝 Week 1 Reflection (Days 1–5)

## 📌 Summary
Week 1 focused on building foundational statistical understanding, covering data classification, measures of central tendency, variability, quartiles, percentiles, and distribution analysis.

---

## 📚 Key Learnings

### 🧩 Data Classification
Developed the ability to distinguish between qualitative and quantitative variables, and further classify quantitative variables as discrete or continuous.

### 📍 Central Tendency
Learned how mean, median, and mode describe the center of a dataset and when each measure is most appropriate.

### 📈 Variability
Understood how range, variance, and standard deviation quantify spread and consistency within datasets.

### 📊 Quartiles & Percentiles
Learned how to segment distributions into ranked portions and interpret relative position within a dataset.

### 📉 Distribution & Skewness
Developed understanding of symmetric vs skewed distributions and how skewness affects statistical interpretation.

---

## ⚠️ Challenges Faced
- Interpreting when to use mean vs median in skewed datasets.
- Understanding the practical significance of variance versus standard deviation.
- Learning how distribution shape influences business interpretation.

---

## 💡 Key Takeaway
Averages alone are insufficient for analysis; understanding spread and distribution is equally critical for meaningful interpretation.
