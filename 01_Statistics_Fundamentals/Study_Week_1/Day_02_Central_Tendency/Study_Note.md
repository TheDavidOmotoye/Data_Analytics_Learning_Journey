# 📘 Day 2 Study Note — Mean, Median & Mode

## 🎯 Objective
Learn measures of central tendency and understand the effect of outliers.

---

## 🧠 Key Concepts

## 1️⃣ Mean
The average value.

Formula:
Mean = Σx / n

Excel Formula:
=AVERAGE(range)

---

## 2️⃣ Median
The middle value after sorting data.

Excel Formula:
=MEDIAN(range)

---

## 3️⃣ Mode
The most frequently occurring value.

Excel Formula:
=MODE.SNGL(range)

---

## 📊 Key Observation

Dataset with outlier:
- Mean increased significantly
- Median changed slightly
- Mode remained unchanged

---

## 📌 Important Insight

Why Mean Changes More:
Outliers heavily influence the mean because every value contributes to the calculation.

Median is more resistant to extreme values.

---

## 📈 Result Interpretation

| Measure | Normal Data | With Outlier |
|---|---|---|
| Mean | 136.36 | 150.12 |
| Median | 136 | 138 |
| Mode | 134 | 134 |

---

## 🔍 Key Insight

When data contains outliers:
- Median is often more reliable than mean.
- Mean may become misleading.

---

## 📝 Reflection

Central tendency helps summarize datasets quickly. Today showed that choosing the correct statistical measure matters greatly when data contains extreme values.
