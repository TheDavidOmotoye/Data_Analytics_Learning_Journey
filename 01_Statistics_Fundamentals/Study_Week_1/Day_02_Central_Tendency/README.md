# 📍 Day 2 — Measures of Central Tendency

## 📘 Objective
Learn how to identify the central value of a dataset using mean, median, and mode.

---

## 📚 Concepts Covered
- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Appropriate use cases for each metric

---

## 🛠 Analysis Performed
- Calculated mean, median, and mode for practice datasets
- Compared central tendency measures across varying distributions
- Evaluated when median is preferable to mean

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Mean provides the arithmetic average of a dataset.
- Median is more robust in skewed distributions and outlier-heavy data.
- Mode helps identify the most frequently occurring value/category.

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaway
No single measure of central tendency is universally best; the correct choice depends on dataset structure and distribution.
