# 📉 Day 5 — Distribution & Skewness

## 📘 Objective
Understand how data is distributed and how skewness affects interpretation.

---

## 📚 Concepts Covered
- Distribution Shapes
- Positive Skew
- Negative Skew
- Symmetrical Distribution
- Histogram Interpretation

---

## 🛠 Analysis Performed
- Created histogram for salary distribution dataset
- Compared mean and median to assess skewness
- Interpreted distribution shape visually and statistically

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Salary distribution exhibited positive skew due to a small number of high earners.
- In skewed distributions, the mean is pulled toward the tail.
- Median often provides a better measure of typical value in skewed datasets.

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaway
Distribution shape influences both statistical interpretation and business conclusions, making visual analysis critical.
