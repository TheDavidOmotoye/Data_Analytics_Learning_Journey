# 📘 Day 5 Study Note — Distribution & Skewness

## 🎯 Objective
Understand data shape and skewness using histograms.

---

## 🧠 Key Concepts

## Distribution
Shows how values spread across a dataset.

---

## Skewness

### Positive Skew
Tail extends to the right.

Characteristics:
- Mean > Median
- Presence of large extreme values

---

## 📊 Dataset Observation

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Mean | 3343.33 |
| Median | 2750 |

Since:
Mean > Median

The distribution is positively skewed.

---

## 📌 IQR Outlier Formula

Lower Bound:
Q1 − 1.5(IQR)

Upper Bound:
Q3 + 1.5(IQR)

---

## 💻 Excel Functions

=AVERAGE(range)
=MEDIAN(range)
=QUARTILE.INC(range,1)
=QUARTILE.INC(range,3)

## 🔍 Key Insight

Histograms reveal:
- Data concentration
- Spread
