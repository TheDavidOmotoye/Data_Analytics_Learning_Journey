# 📝 Week 2 Reflection (Days 6–10)

## 📌 Summary
Week 2 focused on applying statistical concepts to practical analysis through outlier detection, correlation, probability, visualization, and a mini exploratory analysis project.

---

## 📚 Key Learnings

### 🚨 Outlier Detection
Learned to identify unusual observations using the IQR method and interpret their business significance.

### 🔗 Correlation
Developed understanding of variable relationships, including strength, direction, and business implications.

### 📊 Data Visualization
Learned how to select the appropriate chart type based on analytical objectives.

### 🎲 Probability
Understood simple and conditional probability for decision-making and predictive reasoning.

### 🧠 Mini Project Application
Applied all prior statistical concepts in a workforce analytics mini-project to derive actionable insights.

---

## ⚠️ Challenges Faced
- Manual correlation calculation and understanding its formula mechanics.
- Distinguishing between correlation and causation.
- Synthesizing multiple statistical outputs into coherent business insights.

---

## 💡 Key Takeaway
Statistical techniques become truly valuable when integrated into complete exploratory analysis and business storytelling.
