## 📊 Major Project Findings

### 📈 Revenue & Operational Findings
- Revenue demonstrated substantial volatility across the 10-year period.
- Moving averages revealed smoother long-term operational behavior.
- The business demonstrated moderate long-term stability rather than aggressive expansion.

---

### 🔗 Correlation Findings
- Marketing Spend and Sales Revenue showed extremely strong positive correlation (`r = 0.957`).
- Website Traffic and Customer Orders demonstrated strong positive correlation (`r = 0.918`).
- Sales Calls showed minimal direct correlation with Sales Revenue.

---

### 📉 Regression Findings
- Marketing Spend significantly predicted Sales Revenue.
- Sales Calls became significant within the regression model.
- The regression model explained approximately `93.7%` of revenue variation (`R² = 0.937`).

---

### 📅 Seasonal Findings
- Q2 produced the strongest operational performance.
- Q4 consistently demonstrated weaker revenue behavior.
- Seasonal fluctuations meaningfully affected business performance.

---

### 📈 Yearly Trend Findings
- Revenue declined between 2016–2019.
- Operational recovery occurred during 2020–2021.
- Recent years demonstrated moderate stabilization.
