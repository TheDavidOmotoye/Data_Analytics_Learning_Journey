# 📊 Key Insights

## 📈 Revenue & Operational Performance
- Revenue exhibited substantial monthly volatility across the 10-year period.
- Moving averages revealed smoother underlying long-term performance trends.
- The business demonstrated moderate long-term stability rather than aggressive growth.

---

## 📊 Correlation Insights
- Marketing Spend and Sales Revenue showed a very strong positive correlation (`r = 0.957`).
- Website Traffic and Customer Orders also demonstrated strong positive correlation (`r = 0.918`).
- Sales Calls showed almost no meaningful relationship with Sales Revenue (`r ≈ 0.006`).

---

## 📉 Regression Insights
- Marketing Spend significantly predicted Sales Revenue.
- Sales Calls also contributed significantly within the regression model.
- Website Traffic was less statistically significant after controlling for other variables.
- The regression model explained approximately `93.7%` of revenue variation (`R² = 0.937`).

---

## 📅 Seasonal Insights
- Q2 produced the highest average revenue performance.
- Q4 consistently demonstrated the weakest average revenue performance.
- Seasonal fluctuations meaningfully influenced operational outcomes.

---

## 📈 Year-over-Year Insights
- Revenue performance declined between 2016–2019.
- Operational recovery occurred during 2020–2021.
- Recent years demonstrated relative stabilization around the 100k revenue range.
