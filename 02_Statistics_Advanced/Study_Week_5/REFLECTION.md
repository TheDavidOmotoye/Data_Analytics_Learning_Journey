## 📊 Key Learning Experiences

---

## ✅ Day 21 — Regression Fundamentals

This lesson introduced the foundation of predictive modeling using simple linear regression.

Learned how:
- one independent variable predicts an outcome,
- regression lines are interpreted,
- trendlines explain relationships,
- and R² measures model strength.

Built:
- scatter plots,
- trendlines,
- regression equations,
- and regression output interpretation.

Big realization:
> regression is not just about equations — it is about explaining business relationships using data.

---

## ✅ Day 22 — Multiple Regression

This lesson expanded regression modeling by introducing multiple explanatory variables.

Learned how:
- multiple factors jointly influence outcomes,
- coefficients are interpreted,
- p-values measure statistical significance,
- and regression models support business decision-making.

One major learning moment was understanding:
- significant vs insignificant variables,
- and how not all predictors meaningfully affect outcomes.

This improved critical analytical thinking substantially.

---

## ✅ Day 23 — Regression Diagnostics

This was one of the most practical and challenging lessons.

Learned how analysts evaluate whether a model is:
- reliable,
- stable,
- accurate,
- or potentially misleading.

Worked with:
- residuals,
- absolute residuals,
- squared residuals,
- MSE,
- RMSE,
- and outlier detection.

The forecasting and diagnostic charts made the learning feel much closer to real-world business analytics work.

Big insight:
> building a model is not enough — analysts must also evaluate how well the model performs.

---

## ✅ Day 24 — Time Series Basics

This lesson introduced trend analysis across time-based data.

Learned how to:
- identify long-term trends,
- analyze seasonality,
- calculate growth rates,
- apply moving averages,
- and interpret correlation relationships.

Built:
- line charts,
- scatter plots,
- pivot summaries,
- and trend analyses.

This lesson strengthened understanding of:
- trend behavior,
- business growth patterns,
- and time-based analytics.

---

## ✅ Day 25 — Moving Averages & Forecasting Intro

This lesson introduced foundational forecasting concepts.

Learned how:
- moving averages smooth volatility,
- forecasts estimate future outcomes,
- and forecast errors evaluate prediction quality.

Built:
- 3-week moving averages,
- 4-week moving averages,
- forecast models,
- forecast error analysis,
- and forecasting dashboards.

One important realization was understanding that:
> forecasting is never perfect — analysts focus on minimizing error and understanding volatility.

This lesson felt like the true introduction into predictive business analytics.
