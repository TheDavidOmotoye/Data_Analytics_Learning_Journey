# 📊 Key Insights — Day 25: Moving Averages & Forecasting Intro

## 1️⃣ Revenue Trend Insight
Weekly revenue demonstrated a strong upward trend across the 30-week observation period, indicating sustained business growth and improving sales performance.

---

## 2️⃣ Trendline Insight
The linear trendline equation:

y = 665.07x + 24807

shows that revenue increased by approximately $665 weekly on average.

---

## 3️⃣ Trend Strength Insight
The trendline R² value of 0.7985 indicates that approximately 79.85% of revenue variation is explained by time progression and overall business growth trend.

---

## 4️⃣ Moving Average Insight
Both the 3-week and 4-week moving averages closely followed actual revenue patterns, confirming that the business exhibits relatively stable operational performance.

---

## 5️⃣ Forecast Stability Insight
Forecast errors remained moderate across most weeks, suggesting that the moving average forecasting model produced reasonably reliable short-term forecasts.

---

## 6️⃣ Volatility Insight
Several weeks experienced larger forecast deviations, particularly during sudden revenue spikes or declines. This demonstrates the lagging nature of moving averages during periods of rapid change.

---

## 7️⃣ 3-Week vs 4-Week Moving Average Insight
The 3-week moving average reacted faster to short-term fluctuations, while the 4-week moving average produced smoother long-term trend estimates.

---

## 8️⃣ Forecast Error Insight
Positive forecast errors indicated weeks where actual revenue exceeded expectations, while negative forecast errors represented weeks where forecasts overestimated revenue.

---

## 9️⃣ Business Interpretation
The dataset suggests that the business is experiencing:
- stable growth,
- manageable volatility,
- improving revenue consistency,
- and predictable short-term operational performance.

---

## 🔟 Forecasting Limitation Insight
Although moving averages provide effective smoothing and trend estimation, they may respond slowly to sudden market shifts, seasonal spikes, or unexpected operational changes.
