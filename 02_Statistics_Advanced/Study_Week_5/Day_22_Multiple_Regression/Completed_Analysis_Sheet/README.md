## Key Insights

1. The multiple regression model explained approximately 67.8% of employee performance variation.

2. The regression model was statistically significant with a Significance F value far below 0.05.

3. Years of Experience significantly improved employee performance scores.

4. Training Hours had the strongest positive statistical impact on employee performance.

5. Age showed a weak negative relationship with performance and was not statistically significant.

6. Monthly Income was not a statistically significant predictor of employee performance.

7. Employee development factors such as training and experience appear more influential than compensation alone.

8. The regression model demonstrated that multiple predictors together provide stronger forecasting ability than single-variable analysis.

9. The analysis supports the importance of continuous employee training programs.

10. The project demonstrates practical application of predictive analytics in HR performance evaluation.
