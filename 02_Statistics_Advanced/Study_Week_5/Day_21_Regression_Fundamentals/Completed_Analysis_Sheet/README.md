## Key Insights

1. Marketing Spend has an extremely strong positive relationship with Sales Revenue.

2. The regression model achieved an R² value of approximately 0.94, meaning about 94% of sales revenue variation is explained by marketing spend.

3. The regression equation showed that every additional ₦1 spent on marketing generates approximately ₦4.31 in sales revenue.

4. The p-value for Marketing Spend was significantly below 0.05, confirming that marketing spend is a statistically significant predictor of revenue.

5. The scatter plot displayed a clear upward linear trend, visually confirming the strong relationship between marketing investment and revenue growth.

6. The Multiple R value (~0.97) indicates an extremely strong correlation between the predictor and outcome variables.

7. The ANOVA F-statistic (~585) confirmed that the overall regression model is statistically significant.

8. The model residuals appear relatively stable, suggesting the regression line fits the data effectively.

9. Regions with higher lead generation generally showed stronger revenue outcomes, suggesting operational efficiency may influence sales performance.

10. The regression analysis demonstrates how predictive analytics can support marketing budgeting and revenue forecasting decisions.
