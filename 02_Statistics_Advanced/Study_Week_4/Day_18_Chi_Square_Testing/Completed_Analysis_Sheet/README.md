# 📊 Day 18 — Key Insights

---

## 💡 Key Analytical Findings

- Chi-Square testing was used to evaluate whether categorical HR variables were statistically related.

- The analysis focused on the relationship between:
  - Overtime
  - Attrition

---

## 📊 Statistical Result

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| p-value | 0.0598 |
| Alpha | 0.05 |

Since:

```text
0.0598 > 0.05
```

the null hypothesis was not rejected.

---

## 📌 Main Interpretation

The analysis did not find statistically significant evidence that overtime and attrition were related within this dataset.

---

## 📈 Observational Insight

Although overtime employees appeared to show relatively higher attrition frequency, the observed difference was not statistically strong enough to confirm a reliable relationship at the 5% significance level.

---

## 🧠 HR Analytics Insight

Employee attrition may be influenced by additional factors beyond overtime exposure, including:
- job satisfaction
- promotion opportunities
- departmental workload
- management quality
- workplace environment

---

## 📊 Statistical Learning Insight

This lesson demonstrated that:
- visible patterns alone are not enough for analytical conclusions
- statistical testing is necessary to confirm whether observed relationships are meaningful

---

## ⚠ Important Reminder

Chi-Square testing identifies whether categorical variables are statistically related but does not:
- measure relationship strength
- explain causation
- identify operational drivers directly

---

## 🌍 Real-Life Business Implication

Organizations should avoid assuming that overtime alone causes employee attrition without stronger statistical evidence and broader workforce analysis.
