# 📊 Day 19 — Key Insights

---

## 💡 Key Analytical Findings

- Z-score analysis was used to standardize applicant final scores and identify unusually high or low performers.

- The applicant performance distribution appeared relatively balanced and approximately normal, with most z-scores clustering around the mean.

- Most applicants fell within the normal performance range between:
  
```text
-1 and +1
```

indicating moderate consistency in candidate performance.
