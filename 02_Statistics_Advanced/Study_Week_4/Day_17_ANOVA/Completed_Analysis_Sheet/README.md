# 📊 ANOVA Insights

- ANOVA analysis revealed statistically significant productivity differences across departments (p = 0.027).

- Operations recorded the highest average productivity score (87.15), suggesting stronger operational efficiency and workflow performance.

- Support recorded the lowest average productivity score (76.53), indicating possible operational bottlenecks, staffing limitations, or workflow inefficiencies.

- Finance also demonstrated relatively lower productivity compared to Operations, IT, and Sales departments.

- The statistically significant ANOVA result suggests that departmental structure, operational processes, training exposure, or management practices may influence employee productivity outcomes.

- Although significant differences were detected, ANOVA does not identify the exact department pairs responsible for those differences. Additional post-hoc testing would be required for deeper comparison analysis.

- The analysis demonstrates how ANOVA helps organizations compare performance across multiple operational groups simultaneously.
