# 📊 Key Insights — Day 16: T-Tests

- Average customer spending increased from **148.33** to **172.00** after campaign exposure.

- The average spending increase across customers was approximately **23.67 units**.

- The paired t-test produced an extremely small p-value (**p < 0.001**), confirming that the increase in customer spending after campaign exposure was statistically significant.

- Independent t-test analysis showed that the Treatment group outperformed the Control group by a statistically significant margin (**p < 0.001**).

- Customers exposed to the campaign consistently demonstrated stronger spending improvement than customers who were not exposed.

- The one-sample t-test showed that although spending improved significantly, the post-campaign average spending level (**172**) remained significantly below the assumed business target benchmark of **200**.

- The analysis demonstrates that statistical significance does not always imply full business success; a campaign may improve performance while still failing to meet strategic organizational targets.

# 🧠 Final Business Interpretation

The campaign successfully improved customer spending behavior and Treatment customers significantly outperformed the Control group.
