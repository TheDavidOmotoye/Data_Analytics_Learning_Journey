# 📊 Day 12 — Variance, Standard Deviation & Covariance Analysis

## Objective
Analyze variance, standard deviation, covariance, and correlation across business metrics to understand individual volatility and multivariable relationships.

---

## Dataset Overview
Business performance dataset containing:

- Marketing Spend  
- Sales Revenue  
- Website Traffic  
- Customer Complaints  
- Units Sold  
- Discount Rate  

---

## Techniques Applied
- Variance Calculation  
- Standard Deviation Analysis  
- Covariance Analysis  
- Correlation Comparison  

---

## Key Findings

### Revenue Drivers
- **Units Sold** was the strongest direct driver of Sales Revenue (**r = 0.975**).
- **Marketing Spend** strongly aligned with Sales Revenue (**r = 0.966**).
- **Website Traffic** also showed a strong positive relationship with Sales Revenue.

---

### Customer / Operational Insights
- Customer complaints declined moderately as sales volume increased.
- Higher website traffic did not correspond with more complaints.
- Operational performance appears scalable despite increased demand.

---

### Discount Strategy Insights
- Discounts moderately improved Units Sold.
- Discounts also showed a positive relationship with Sales Revenue.
- Pricing strategy appears to support revenue generation without materially harming performance.

---

## Statistical Interpretation
This analysis demonstrated the layered relationship between dispersion and multivariable interaction:

| Metric | Role in Analysis |
|---|---|
| **Variance** | Measured standalone spread/volatility of each metric |
| **Standard Deviation** | Converted spread into interpretable original units |
| **Covariance** | Identified directional joint movement between variables |
| **Correlation** | Standardized covariance into comparable relationship strength |

---

## Business Conclusion
Business metrics moved in logically consistent and statistically meaningful ways.

The analysis showed that:

- Revenue growth is primarily driven by sales volume and marketing investment  
- Website traffic contributes meaningfully to commercial performance  
- Discounting positively influences sales outcomes  
- Operational quality remains stable as business volume scales
