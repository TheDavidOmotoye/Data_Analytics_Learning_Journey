# 📈 Day 11 — Advanced Correlation Analysis

## 📌 Objective
Evaluate relationships between academic performance and influencing factors using advanced correlation techniques:

- Pearson Product-Moment Correlation Coefficient  
- CORREL()  
- PEARSON()  
- Spearman Rank Correlation  
- Scatter Plot Visualization  

---

## 🧠 Core Concepts Applied

### Pearson Product-Moment Correlation Coefficient
Measures the **strength and direction of linear relationship** between two continuous variables.

Also commonly referred to as:

> **Pearson Correlation** or **Pearson’s r**

---

### CORREL()
Excel implementation of Pearson Product-Moment Correlation.

---

### PEARSON()
Alternative Excel implementation of Pearson Product-Moment Correlation.

---

### Spearman Rank Correlation
Measures the **strength and direction of monotonic relationship** between ranked variables.

---

### Scatter Plot
Visual diagnostic used to assess:
- Trend Direction  
- Strength of Association  
- Linearity  
- Outliers / Clustering  

---

## 📌 Key Methodology Note
Pearson Product-Moment Correlation was selected to evaluate linear relationships because all predictor variables were continuous and no significant outliers were detected during exploratory analysis.
