# 🔑 Key Insights

---

## 📊 Hypothesis Testing Insights

- Hypothesis testing evaluates whether observed business changes are statistically meaningful.
- Analysts use p-values to determine whether evidence is strong enough to reject the null hypothesis.
- A statistically significant result suggests the observed effect is unlikely to occur by random chance alone.

---

## 📈 Sales Performance Insights

- Average daily sales increased significantly after campaign implementation.
- The average sales increase across branches was approximately 550 units.
- The extremely small p-value indicated very strong statistical evidence supporting campaign effectiveness.

---

## 📣 Campaign-Type Insights

- Email campaigns produced the strongest average sales improvement.
- Influencer campaigns performed almost identically to Email campaigns.
- Referral campaigns generated the weakest average increase.
- Discount campaign interpretation should remain cautious due to limited observations.

---

## 📊 Statistical Decision Insight

Because:

p-value < 0.05

the null hypothesis was rejected.

This confirmed that campaign-driven sales improvements were statistically significant.

---

## 🧠 Business Interpretation

The analysis demonstrated that:
- Sales increases alone are not sufficient evidence of campaign effectiveness.
- Analysts must determine whether observed changes are statistically meaningful.
- Hypothesis testing supports evidence-based business recommendations.
