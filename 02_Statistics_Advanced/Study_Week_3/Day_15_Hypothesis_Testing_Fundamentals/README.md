# 📊 Day 15 — Hypothesis Testing Fundamentals

---

## 🎯 Objective

Test business claims using null and alternative hypotheses and determine whether observed sales changes are statistically significant.

---

## 📚 Dataset Overview

The dataset contained:
- Average daily sales before campaigns
- Average daily sales after campaigns
- Campaign types
- Sample sizes
- Target sales benchmarks

The analysis focused on evaluating whether marketing campaigns significantly improved branch sales performance.

---

## 🛠 Analytical Techniques Applied

- Sales Difference Analysis
- Null & Alternative Hypothesis Formulation
- Paired t-Test
- P-Value Evaluation
- Statistical Decision-Making
- Campaign-Type Comparison
- Business Interpretation

---

## 📊 Key Findings

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Avg Sales Before | 6589.20 |
| Avg Sales After | 7139.51 |
| Avg Sales Change | 550.31 |
| P-Value | 0.0000009 |

---

## 📌 Statistical Decision

Because:

```text
0.0000009 < 0.05
```

the null hypothesis was rejected.

This indicated extremely strong statistical evidence that the campaigns significantly increased average daily sales.

---

## 📈 Campaign Performance Summary

| Campaign Type | Avg Sales Difference |
|---|---:|
| Email | 610.50 |
| Influencer | 609.67 |
| Discount | 490.00 |
| Referral | 431.09 |

---

## 🔥 Business Insights

- Email and Influencer campaigns generated the strongest sales improvements.
- Referral campaigns produced weaker average gains.
- Discount campaign results require additional observations for stronger reliability.
- The observed sales increase was statistically meaningful rather than random.

---

## 🌍 Real-Life Applications

This type of analysis is widely used in:
- Marketing campaign evaluation
- Product testing
- Employee performance analysis
- Financial strategy testing
- Healthcare treatment evaluation

Organizations use hypothesis testing to support evidence-based decision-making.

---

## 🧠 Key Analytical Principle

Hypothesis testing helps analysts distinguish between:
- Random fluctuations
- Meaningful business change

The lesson demonstrated how statistical evidence supports more reliable strategic business decisions.
